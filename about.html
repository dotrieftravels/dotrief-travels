WHO WE ARE

Your Trusted Partner for Travel & Global Opportunities

Dotrief Travels Ltd is a professional travel and education consultancy committed to helping individuals, families and businesses access reliable travel solutions. From affordable flight bookings and visa assistance to study abroad opportunities and vacation planning, we simplify international travel with expert guidance and personalized service.

Our Mission

To connect people with global opportunities by providing professional, reliable and affordable travel, education and immigration support while delivering exceptional customer service.

Our Vision

To become one of Africa's most trusted travel and study abroad brands, recognized for integrity, innovation and customer satisfaction.

OUR STORY

Our Journey

Dotrief Travels was established with a simple vision—to make international travel, education and global opportunities more accessible through honest guidance and dependable service.


From helping travellers book flights to supporting students with international admissions and guiding clients through visa preparation, our focus has always been on delivering personalised solutions rather than one-size-fits-all services.


Today, Dotrief Travels continues to serve individuals, families, professionals and organisations by connecting them with opportunities across the world while maintaining professionalism, transparency and customer satisfaction.
